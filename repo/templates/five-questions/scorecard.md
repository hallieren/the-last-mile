**Scoring Discipline** (full rules in 7.2):

- Score each question 1–5, anchors are in Template 7.1. **A blank evidence cell always scores 2, no exceptions.** Only numbers, direct quotes, and data samples count as evidence. Adjectives do not.
- Any question scoring 2 or below eliminates the candidate outright, no weighting, no averaging. The five questions multiply, and a factor near zero makes the whole product zero.
- A score of 3 is a conditional pass. Write the condition in the field above with a deadline. If it is not met by then, the score drops to 2.
