# deployment-charter, Deployment Charter Scaffold

Corresponding template: Template 4, Deployment Charter and Pre-mortem Memo, sections 4.1–4.2 (companion: Chapter 4) ([full appendix](../../../docs/appendices/template-04-deployment-charter.md)).
Purpose: a fillable one-page scaffold for the charter's seven elements (with a self-check line for each), plus an AI prompt that turns contracting meeting minutes into a charter draft.

| File | What it is | How to use |
|------|--------|--------|
| charter.md | The seven-element document scaffold (Template 4.1, with every self-check line) | Copy it, pre-fill the seven elements, bring it to the contracting meeting, revise together, and have both sides sign |
| prompt-minutes-to-charter.md | Prompt: meeting minutes to a charter draft | Paste in the de-identified minutes, take the draft, fill it out against the "still to discuss" list, then send it |

The 60-minute contracting meeting agenda and the before/after checklists are in the Template 4.2 text. The example text comes from the book's Anchor & Helm case.
