## Fear/Win Prompt Library (Prompts, Not Answers, Replace Every Line With Field Evidence)

| Role | Common Fear | Common Win |
|------|----------|----------|
| Decision-maker | The project becomes his failure; cannot give the people above him a number | A number and a story he can tell outward; the "mastered AI" label |
| Actual user | Getting replaced; taking the blame for AI's mistakes; having experience ignored; being monitored | Less repetitive work; his judgment written into the system under his name |
| Data owner | A definition problem gets pinned on him; poor data quality gets exposed | Someone fixes a data definition for him |
| Risk owner | Being bypassed, the last to know; an unauditable black box | A project that puts risk on page one; a case of prudence he can show upward |
| Maintainer | Inheriting a black box; an undocumented mess that becomes his problem later | New skills; a sense of ownership over the code; co-build credit |
| Blocker | Different every time, but something he is protecting always sits behind the blocking | What he is protecting gets written into the plan explicitly |

Reminder for AI projects: if the actual user's fear cell does not name "getting replaced" or "taking the blame for AI," the likely reason is you have not asked, not that he has no fear.

## Two Questions to Answer Once the Map Is Drawn

1. Which name has never been in the meeting room? (a blind spot)
2. Which cell's content did you guess? (go verify it. Guessing wrong on "what they fear" is more dangerous than leaving it blank.)
