# role-charter, Deliverer Role Charter

Corresponding template: Template 2, Deliverer Role Charter (companion: Chapter 2) ([full appendix](../../../docs/appendices/template-02-role-charter.md)).
Purpose: a one-page role charter written within the first week of kickoff, aligned with the sponsor item by item in 15–30 minutes.

| File | What it is | How to use |
|------|--------|--------|
| role-charter.md | A fillable one-page template (four sections, 2.1–2.4) | Copy it, replace the placeholders, read it to the sponsor item by item |
| prompt-role-charter.md | A fill-in facilitation prompt | Paste it into an AI chat, answer three boundary questions, then take the draft |

Sample text comes from the book's Anchor & Helm case or placeholders.
