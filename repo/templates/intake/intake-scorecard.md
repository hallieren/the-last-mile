# Intake Red Line Checklist + Five-Dimension Scorecard (Template 25.1 / 25.2)

**Order (fixed): red lines first, scoring second.** Any one of them vetoes. A red line is not scored, not weighted, and not compensated by any high score. Any one trigger stops things right there and sends the candidate straight into the saying-no process (Template 25.3) and the kill register (`kill-log.csv`). Scoring can still happen after a trigger, but for one purpose only, seeing which dimensions an alternative path would need to fix.

## Step One: Red Line Checklist (25.2, Any One Vetoes)

| # | Red Line | Test Question (One Line) | Triggered? | Evidence |
|---|------|------------------|--------|------|
| 1 | Automated decisions at an irreversible-harm step | Can the harm from the single worst output be taken back? | _ | ____ |
| 2 | A move of decision rights with no human oversight capacity behind it | After the move, is there still someone with the time to look, the ability to judge, and the authority to stop it? | _ | ____ |
| 3 | Outward-facing output in a regulatory grey zone | When that output causes a dispute, what do you answer the regulator with? | _ | ____ |

Red line discipline: additions and removals are not decided by a project team vote. They are reconsidered only when an external source changes (regulatory guidance turns clear, oversight capacity gets built, a reversal mechanism appears), and each reconsideration leaves a trail. Keep the list short. Every "fake red line" you add, something that could really just be scored, dilutes the veto power of the real ones.

## Step Two: Five-Dimension Scorecard (25.1, Reached Only If No Red Line Triggered)

Scoring rule: 1 to 5, weakest-link logic. Any dimension at 2 or below does not enter the schedule, write a re-evaluation condition instead. A 3 is a conditional pass, with the condition and the deadline written into the table. No weighting, no averaging. The evidence column takes only numbers, users' own words, and samples.

| Dimension | Test Question | Score (1–5) | Evidence (Numbers / Users' Own Words / Samples) | Condition and Deadline (Required at a Score of 3) |
|------|----------|-----------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| Strategic value | Win it, and what does the organization get? One department's thanks, or agenda power and standard-setting power over a class of problem? | _ | ____ | ____ |
| Data readiness | How far do the key data exist, how reachable are they, how far can they be trusted? Have they been reconciled? | _ | ____ | ____ |
| Owner in place | Is there someone on the business side who signs for the outcome? Who backstops the errors, who maintains it after launch? | _ | ____ | ____ |
| Production path | Do review, oversight, and change discipline get through? Or can only the demo live? | _ | ____ | ____ |
| Reuse potential | What gets banked into the pattern library? How much cheaper is the second delivery? | _ | ____ | ____ |

**Verdict**: Take it / Take it with conditions (condition and deadline: ____) / Do not take it (goes into `kill-log.csv`, write the revival condition as an event, not a date).
