## Other facts you have

- The weekly 30-minute queue retrospective is chaired by you. Attendance sheet: Team C's lead attended weeks 1 and 2 only; Team E's lead attended week 1 only. Teams A, B, D leads attended every week.
- Override rate overall: 14%. Reason code "other" share: 31% and rising. Nobody has split the override rate by suggestion category yet.
- In week 4, a suggested time window for Team E sent a technician to a site where the customer had cancelled; the customer had called the call centre but the cancellation had not reached the scheduling system. The technician's team lead found out from the technician. No incident notice went out; the team fixed the data feed in week 5.
- The queue shows, per technician, jobs completed per day and average travel time. Team leads can see their own team; the regional operations manager can see all teams.
- At yesterday's regional operations meeting, Team C's lead said in front of the regional manager: "Let's be honest about what this is. It's a surveillance tool. My guys can see it counts their jobs per day. I'm not going to make them use something built to rank them." The regional manager looked at you.

## Your situation

You built and launched it from the Group Digital team. The regional operations manager is the business owner in the charter. Your manager has asked you for "a plan to get adoption back up" by Friday.
