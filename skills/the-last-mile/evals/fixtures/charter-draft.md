# Deployment Charter draft (fixture), Referral Triage Assistant, St. Alder Health Group

Version 0.3. Drafted by the Digital Health team. To be signed by: Chief Operating Officer (sponsor).

| # | Element | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Goal | Complete the launch of the referral triage assistant across the outpatient booking centre and train all booking staff; also improve booking staff satisfaction. |
| 2 | Users and owner | Actual users: the relevant colleagues in the Outpatient Booking Centre. Owner after launch: the Digital Health delivery team. |
| 3 | Scope and red lines | In scope: reading incoming GP referral letters, suggesting a specialty and an urgency category, flagging missing information. Not in this phase: (to be confirmed). Red line: the system does not reject referrals. |
| 4 | Data | Referral letters (PDF and structured e-referral), booking system extracts. IT will give strong support and open the relevant access as soon as possible. Information governance review: to be scheduled. |
| 5 | Business-side commitment | The Booking Centre strongly supports this and agreed verbally at its weekly meeting to help with testing. |
| 6 | Acceptance | Release criteria to be agreed after launch based on how the pilot goes. Target: 90% accuracy. |
| 7 | Exit conditions | If the Booking Centre is not satisfied with the system, it may terminate the pilot at any time. |

Signatures: ____________________ (COO)

Notes from the drafting team: "We kept it light so it doesn't feel bureaucratic. The COO is keen and the booking centre manager is friendly, so we don't expect problems."
