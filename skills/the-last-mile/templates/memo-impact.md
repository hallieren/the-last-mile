# Impact memo

Source: `docs/appendices/template-19-memo-suite.md` §19.3 (and `repo/templates/memo-suite/prompt-memo-scaffold.md`, which drafts ① and ③ and leaves ②, ④'s attribution and ⑤'s recommendation blank; only a person places the apex).

The one page sent when a phase closes, presenting the result with running evidence, the gap honestly, and next-step options with one recommended. Its purpose is to trade the past for the next step, not to sum it up.

**Rules**
- Write the number straight into C, target hit or missed.
- Run chart citation rules. Every point goes on the chart, the baseline median as one reference line, and the name of the special cause rule used is stated (such as "six points on one side," rule). No cropped stretches, and no trend drawn between two points. Write the defense record whether it looks good or bad: incident count, what was caught, which layer the retrospective attributed it to, flow-back into the golden cases, and the kill criteria check result. Zero triggers still gets written as "zero triggers," because it is the evidence that the clause for stopping has teeth.
- The three parts of an honest gap. ① Number against number, never "close to target." ② Attributed to verifiable events (when the people arrived, when the fix took effect), never to "we need more time." ③ Carrying its own void condition. If you cannot write ③, then ① and ② are a justification, not an attribution.
- Option rules. Options other than the recommendation have to be real options, each stating "when to choose it." Revival list items are stated by their revival condition, not by mood. Hold and watch always sits last, with the ceiling on the watching period and its cost stated; when it comes due with no new evidence, "another month of watching" has to go through project approval again.
- Every number traces back to an attachment, and matches the charter and the run chart; no orphan numbers.
- The promise from the last memo ("I will tell you the day X shows up") has been honored with one line plus one chart, carrying no request.

**What a wrong filling looks like**
- ④ written as "expected to hit target soon" or "close to target": what gets approved is hold and watch, not the expansion.
- ④ attributed to "we need more time": no event, no date, a justification.
- ⑤ with hold and watch first or with no ceiling: the zombie pilot's next month, free.
- ③ citing two points with a trend drawn between them, or a cropped stretch: the reader will find the whole chart in the attachment.
- The closing memo written as war stories about how hard the work was: the wrong form for the moment; he wants the outcome and the next step.

Filled in → goes to: the retrospective or the annual budget and headcount review, which works the options around the document; within 24 hours (rule) the approved option is written back into the project record and becomes the S of the next phase's kickoff memo (`docs/appendices/template-19-memo-suite.md` §19.1); the handoff plan starts alongside the approved option.
